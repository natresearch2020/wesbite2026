Our story

Research support since 2015

National Research Consultancy was established in Coimbatore, the Manchester City of Tamil Nadu, in 2015 with the aim of facilitating the research community in producing quality, accurate and effective research on time.

Our original company profile reports support for scholars from multiple disciplines around the world, including assistance with theses, research articles, copy editing, originality review, data analysis, sample collection, literature review, topic selection and publication.

We work with doctorate-qualified subject experts across disciplines. Our approach emphasizes individual attention, review by subject specialists, confidentiality and a systematic workflow.

Historical figures in the original company profile refer to the period when that profile was prepared; they have not been presented here as current totals.
